Subnet Research Reports

Deep-dive reports on the major subnets — emission, momentum, liquidity, validators, development activity, and risk. New reports published monthly. No subscription: buy any report with one $1 USDC payment over x402.

How to buy a report
Each report is a resource on the x402 payment protocol — pay $1 USDC with a wallet signature, get the full markdown report back.
  1. POST /v1/x402/checkout/research:<slug> → 402 + payment requirements
  2. Sign with your wallet (any x402 client SDK, or your own agent)
  3. Retry with PAYMENT-SIGNATURE → 200 + the report

Full endpoint docs and SDKs: subnetroi.com/x402_pay.html
