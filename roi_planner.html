Your setup

Incentive share is your assumed fraction of a subnet's miner emissions — it is positional and competitive (top miners take most; median miners hover near breakeven). This is an assumption, not a live number. Registration cost is a sunk burn.

Methodology

your TAO/day = miners_tao_per_day × incentive_share · your USD/day = your TAO/day × TAO price · net/day = USD/day − power − infra − amortized registration − amortized capex · payback = GPU capex / net/day

miners_tao_per_day is the subnet's total miner pool (live from TaoMarketCap). Miner income is alpha-denominated and volatile; emission is not pro-rata to alpha price (spec-440 gate redistributes to winners). Figures are estimates, not predictions — validate against taostats before acting.
