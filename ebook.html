Here's the truth nobody tells you

When you stake TAO to a subnet, you are not putting it in a savings account. You are swapping your TAO for that subnet's Alpha token. Your wallet shows the Alpha's value in TAO terms — so when the Alpha price drops, your "TAO" drops with it.

That one sentence explains the "losing TAO" posts, the stopped rewards, and the wrecked new-subnet gambles. And almost nobody on Bittensor understands it.
