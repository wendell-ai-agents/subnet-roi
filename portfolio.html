Portfolio Tracker

Log your staked positions — SubnetROI values them live in TAO and USD, and tracks your gain or loss against the Alpha price at entry. Your portfolio is bound to your wallet address; no account, no API key.

🔓 Connect your wallet
Your portfolio is stored under your wallet address (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Tangem extension — any EVM wallet). Connect once to add positions and see live valuations. Programmatic access to this data is $1 USDC via x402.

🔔 Alerts

Get a Telegram nudge when a position moves past your thresholds. Link your Telegram chat ID once — any position that drops or rises past the % threshold triggers a notification (max one per position per day).
Where's my Telegram ID? Message @userinfobot on Telegram — it replies with your numeric ID. Alerts only fire if this is set.
